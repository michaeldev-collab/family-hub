# Child panel moved

On 2026-07-26 the child Waveshare panel firmware and panel API/web surfaces were
archived out of the live Family Hub product.

The previous subsystem was archived outside the public repository. Restore
documentation remains in the private project archive.

Live tree no longer includes child firmware sources/bin or the child PlatformIO
env. Household panel mutations remain limited to chore completion and grocery
toggles. Unused SQLite panel tables were left in place (no destructive
migration).
